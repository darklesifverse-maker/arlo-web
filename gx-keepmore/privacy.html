Keep More — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-05

Keep More ("the App") is designed to work fully offline. This policy explains what happens with your information.

Data we collect

None. The App does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

Your settings and content (for example favorites, history or progress) are saved only on your device using local storage. They never leave your device and are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

Permissions

The App does not request access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files.

Telegram

If you open the App inside Telegram, Telegram processes your use of Telegram under its own privacy policy. Optional support payments in Telegram Stars are processed by Telegram; we only receive the payment amount and an anonymous payment ID.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
